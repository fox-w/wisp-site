Privacy policy

Last updated: August 17, 2026

Wisp is built to be private by design. The short version: we do not collect, store, or share any of your data. At all.

What stays on your device

Everything. The documents you import, your reading positions, your settings, your reading stats, and your goals all live only on your device. Wisp works fully offline. There is no account, no sign up, and no server on our side receiving anything.

What we collect

Nothing. Wisp has no analytics, no trackers, no advertising, and no third party SDKs that phone home. We cannot see what you read, when you read, or that you use Wisp at all.

App Store data

Apple may show us anonymous, aggregate numbers such as download counts and crash reports, governed by your own device settings and Apple's privacy policy. That data comes from Apple, not from anything inside Wisp.

Deleting your data

Delete the app and everything is gone, because it only ever existed on your device.

Changes

If Wisp ever adds an optional feature that needs data to leave your device, such as cloud sync, it will be opt-in, clearly explained here first, and never the default.

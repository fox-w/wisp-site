Support

Breez is reading that works for you: one word at a time, always in the same place, at a pace that is yours.

Get help

Found a bug, have a question, or want a feature? Write to wispreader@outlook.com and a human will reply.

Quick answers

Where is my data? On your device. If you sign in for sync, your library is also kept in your account so it follows you. See the privacy policy.

What can I read? Books, PDF, EPUB, Word, text, Markdown and web pages, pasted text, and free classics from the Browse tab. Breez remembers your place in each one.

The pace feels wrong. Open Settings, then Pace, and move the slider while the preview plays. Long words hold a beat longer, dense passages ease off on their own, and dragging sideways takes you back a few words.

Reading feels tiring. Try Paper Night or another warm theme, bigger letters, or blink rests. Every reading feature has an off switch.

Pro and purchases. Manage or cancel your plan in your iPhone Settings, under your name, then Subscriptions. To restore a purchase, open Breez Pro from the You tab and choose Restore.

Breez was previously called Breezia, and before that Wisp.

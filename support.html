Support

Wisp is reading that works for you: one word at a time, always in the same place, at a pace that is yours.

Get help

Found a bug, have a question, or want a feature? Write to wispreader@outlook.com and a human will reply.

Quick answers

Where is my data? On your device, and nowhere else. Wisp has no accounts and collects nothing. See the privacy policy.

What can I read? Paste any text, or open TXT, Markdown, HTML, PDF, DOCX, and EPUB files. Wisp remembers your place in each one.

The pace feels wrong. Tap the speed controls while reading, or let the onboarding calibration find your pace again from Settings. Long words hold a beat longer, dense passages ease off on their own, and rereading is one touch.

Reading feels tiring. Try the Fade mode, a warmer theme, a bigger text size, or blink rests. Every reading feature has an off switch.
